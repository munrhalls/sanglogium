# Cognitive Load: Lay of the Land Technique

**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Source:** Real-time training session
**Severity:** High
**Frequency:** Systematic (applies to all complex tasks)

## The Technique
- Squeeze brain gently to list relevant info pieces
- Organize with categories if possible
- Aim for < 15 minutes duration
- Treat as puzzle pieces to organize

## Process Steps
1. **Initial Scan** - Gentle information gathering
2. **Categorization** - Group related pieces
3. **Time Box** - Keep under 15 minutes
4. **Organization** - Find patterns and relationships

## Subtle Chunk Detection
- Look for relationship groups
- Identify chunk potential
- Prevent overflow by not taking too much
- Sense where connections form naturally

## Cognitive Load Management
- **Hard Requirement:** Not take too much at once
- Gauge challenge level for optimal engagement
- Map real new relationships
- Push toward what you need to ultimately see

## Application Rules
- **DO:** Start with gentle scanning
- **DON'T:** Force organization prematurely
- **DO:** Keep sessions < 15 minutes
- **DON'T:** Overwhelm with too many pieces
