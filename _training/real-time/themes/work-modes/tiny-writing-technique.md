# Work Modes: Tiny Writing Technique

**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Source:** Real-time training session
**Severity:** High
**Frequency:** Universal (applies to all precision work)

## The Technique
Close attention on a small point to increase frame rate and perceptual acceleration

## Benefits
- **Frame Rate Acceleration:** Increases mental processing speed
- **Calming Effect:** Reduces overwhelm and anxiety
- **Precision Enhancement:** Aids careful, accurate work
- **Focus Lock:** Prevents distraction and thoughts leakage

## Balance Principles
- **Reg Min Distances:** Maintain regular minimum spacing
- **Struggle Level:** Not too challenging, not too easy
- **Continuity:** Sustain the rhythm over time
- **Uniform Motion:** Consistent pace and rhythm

## Application Context
- Web development precision tasks
- Complex problem breakdown
- When feeling overwhelmed
- Need for increased perceptual speed

## Prevention Rules
- **DO:** Zoom in on tiny specific points
- **DON'T:** Try to handle everything at once
- **DO:** Maintain regular rhythm
- **AVOID:** Inconsistent motion patterns
