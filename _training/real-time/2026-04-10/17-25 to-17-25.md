# atomic operations - it means all operations of a given network request to e.g. redis or cms or db, happen as one indivisible unit - they cannot be broken up to wait for something else to happen; if another request happens, it's ignored until the first atomic operation in queue finishes 100%; 
why useful - prevents race conditions; someone reserved 2 of stock when 5 stock is left; 1 milisecond later request cam that reserved 4 of stock; the latter will fail because it will be ignored until 1st finishes 100% all its operations
