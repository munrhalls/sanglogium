# Development Fundamentals - DRAFT

## FUNDAMENTALS to build upon:
- both overview doc and workflow doc must contain absolutely nothing except what is absolutely necessary to achieve target end-state
- both must be developed from most banal simplest sketch and gradually, with iterative verification and checks, go on to addressing real problems at specific trace points with specific ways to solve those problems
- both must be kept simple and 100% direct to end-user interaction and human-verification - the moment they complicate or the linkage or relevance become vague, the entire endeavor sets on downward spiral and fails
- both should use template-like funnel approach, similar to a database schema except much more loose (flexibility is key)
- both must be adaptable and flexible

### 1. End-to-End Macro Simplicity
- you don't start from trying to get end-to-end macro simplicity and effectiveness in one go, you gradually iterate verification and checking the core ideas and relationships from simplest sketch first, to gradually revealing real-world coding challenges and difficulties and specific problems and ways to handle those problems with specific solutions and verifying and checking those specific solutions to confirm the real ones that'll be likely to be effective with direct relevance to the scenario at hand
- you don't do cargo cult architecturing and designing, you do real-time direct scenario and environment and variables and means and tools and macro relationships and flows analysis based on the overall intent and target-state
- gaps closing workflow strategy is critical - it must prevent overwhelm and check that any point of the workflow, there is not too much going at once
- a workflow part or chunk must must not exist unless the directness of human-verification and human bus stops inspection and direct relevance to end-user interaction is 100% preserved
- First achieve end-to-end macro simplicity and effectiveness in terms of pure overview .md document of target end-state folders and files organization verification means (ui + server comps + console logs), tests, actual code chunks
- Achieve end-to-end macro simplicity and effectiveness in terms of pure overview .md document of the effective workflow (effective is only as effective as its weakest link in terms of maintaining simplicity and wholeness at the same time)

### 2. Effective Workflow Principles
- **Directness vs Indirectness**: Any indirectness equals blind spots, which equals risks of slop and drifts, which equals accumulated slop and drifts which quickly result in complicating - and complicating is the failure point after which everything is a subtle downward spiral and time sink of ineffective, disorderly waste
- **Direct Human Verification**: Direct human-verification and code inspection is mandatory, any lacks in there equal blind spots - and blind spots equal the above mentioned downward spiral towards complication, entanglement and ineffectiveness

### 3. Verification Principles
- It's not just UI or just console logs - it's the combination of those that fits pre-chosen verified pattern that works for human verification, given the task's environment and context
- It needs to flow from questioned requirements and contain nothing unnecessary or useless - only that which is needed for complete verification
- It must be kept simple and effective - which translates to directly human-verifiable

### 4. Test Principles
- A test must not exist if it is not anchored in human-verification and end-user interaction
- A test must not exist if its linkage to the human-verification and end-user interaction is vague, indirect or possibly weak and easily corrupted
- A test can only exist if it is direct, clear, simple, specific, complete in terms of contract - and in terms of direct relevance - no cargo cult smell
- A test must not exist if it's useless or questionable value or duplicate or overlap or cargo-cult smell or questionable relevance
- **Cargo cult testing is useless**
- A test must start from human verification anchor - it cannot start from clouds, abstract slop, cargo cult best practice or cargo cult ideation
- A test must be aimed at a specific problem, specific failure, the failure must be anchored in real problem in real user end-interaction, so it must be with the solution-implementation, which then makes the failure no longer appear
