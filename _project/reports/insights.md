# AI, the mirror, our tendencies and mystery

The AI tends to be like a mirror, and it tends to mirror what we communicate in terms of probabilities back to us. What matters about that is the fact that we land somewhere on the spectrum of openness to being closed.

There is this thing about human beings where we have a flawed tendency in that we can have premature collapsing of mystery into bias and falsehood, from our sense of flawed response to the pressure of time and need for speed, for getting the results fast. What matters is that if we do that, then AI will tend to mirror back to us that bias and the premature crystallization, whereas if we are open and explorative, then we tend to stick to reality much more closely and be open and adaptive. And then the AI also tends to mirror that back to us.

Another problem is words. We think that our language describes reality, but in actual reality, language shuts down and obscures our windows onto reality with our preconceptions and premature crystallizations.
