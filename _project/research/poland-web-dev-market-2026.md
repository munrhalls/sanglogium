# Poland Web Development Market 2026 - Source-Level Research

## Salary Data (Source: Pracuj.pl, NoFluffJobs - May 2026)

### B2B Contract Rates
- **Senior Frontend (React/Next.js/TS):** 800-1000 MD B2B (≈8,000-10,000 PLN/month)
- **Senior Fullstack (React/TS/Node + AI):** 140-170 PLN/hour + VAT B2B
- **Mid React Developer:** 13,000-21,000 PLN/month
- **Senior React Developer:** 18,000-28,000 PLN/month
- **Junior React Developer:** 5,000-12,000 PLN/month

### Employment Models
- B2B is standard for senior roles
- UoP (employment contract) available for some positions
- Remote work is common (100% remote or hybrid)

## Stack Requirements (Source: Actual Job Postings)

### Mandatory Technologies
- **React.js** - 100% of postings require
- **TypeScript** - Baseline expectation for senior roles
- **Next.js** - Increasingly required (not optional)
- **Node.js** - Required for fullstack positions
- **Git** - Universal requirement

### High-Value Differentiators
- **Headless CMS Experience:** Sanity, Contentful, Strapi explicitly mentioned in senior postings
- **E-commerce Experience:** Valued for senior roles
- **AI Coding Tools:** Increasingly mentioned (Cursor, Windsurf, AI-assisted development)
- **Design Systems:** Experience building component libraries
- **Tailwind CSS:** Commonly required
- **Testing:** Playwright, Jest, Vitest experience valued

### Senior Role Requirements
- **Minimum 5 years commercial experience** (explicitly stated in senior postings)
- **Greenfield project experience** (building from scratch)
- **Platform/content management system experience**
- **API integration experience**
- **Performance optimization knowledge**
- **Accessibility (a11y) awareness**

## Market Trends (Source: Optiveum, ITCompare - 2025/2026)

### Junior Market Reality
- Market no longer absorbs juniors at scale
- Companies prioritize: shorter time-to-productivity, practical skills, tech stack readiness
- Shift toward structured internships, trainee programs, hybrid positions with KPIs
- Generic "junior" openings decreasing

### Identity Fraud Concerns
- Rise of "fake CVs" and identity fraud
- Candidates constructing "local" personas to bypass screening
- Video verification now mandatory first step
- AI-generated cover letters and CVs becoming common

### Recruitment Process
- 2-4 interviews typical (candidates prefer ≤2)
- Technical tests becoming standard (Codility, HackerRank, TestDome)
- Pair programming and take-home assignments common
- AI-based pre-screening gaining ground (24% distrust AI selection)
