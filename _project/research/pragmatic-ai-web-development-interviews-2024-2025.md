## Key Themes Across Interviews

1. **AI as Force Multiplier, Not Replacement** - Consensus that AI augments developers rather than replacing them
2. **Shift to AI-Native Development** - Transition from AI-assisted to AI-first workflows
3. **Importance of Fundamentals** - Strong engineering fundamentals remain critical for directing AI tools effectively
4. **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** - Emerging standard for AI-tool integration
5. **Agentic AI** - Move from code completion to autonomous AI agents
6. **Changing Developer Roles** - Evolution from pure coding to architecture, review, and AI orchestration
