# racing but not rushing
Every second compounds. If not racing this second, not racing the next, then minute, hour, day passes with nothing done. Compound effect either slackens to nothing or tightens to coherent done.

# expressing my highest potential but not forcing
Forcing equals rushing. Rushing equals incoherent, uncoordinated, disorganized panic running to nowhere. Not letting it happen.

# letting but not dragging
Letting so much that it's slack covered under unicorn running the clouds. Letting equals Connor McGregor in the ring - serene look on his face but underneath, it's all aggression, alert and calm.
