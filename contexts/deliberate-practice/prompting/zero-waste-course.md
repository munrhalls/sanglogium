# Deliberate Practice: Zero-Waste Prompting

## Level 1: "State Zero" Interrogation (Data vs. Adjectives)
**Principle:** Never prompt blindly. Replace human adjectives with machine data.
**Context:** You have a new asset, hero_bg.png. It looks "too heavy" and "muddied."
**The Drill:** Before opening the prompt window, what is the exact PowerShell or ImageMagick CLI command you run to establish the mathematical reality (dimensions, size, color depth, alpha channel)?

---

## Level 2: The Physics Boundary (Input/Output vs. Tooling)
**Principle:** Define physics (Input/Output), do not constrain tooling unless you have 100% mechanical awareness.
**Context:** You have a logo.webp (800x600). You need it to be exactly 400x400, centered, no stretching.
**The Drill:** Write the Zero-Waste prompt. Define the exact Input State and the exact Required Output State without naming a single ImageMagick flag.

---

## Level 3: The Observable Feedback Loop (Logs vs. Emotion)
**Principle:** Force AI to correct mathematical logic, not guess visual outcomes.
**Context:** You ran an AI script to compress a video. The output is a black screen with audio.
**The Drill:** What is your immediate next action? How do you extract the failure data, and what do you feed back to the AI for a 1-to-1 mathematical correction?
