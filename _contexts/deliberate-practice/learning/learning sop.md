# SOP: First-Principles Learning (The Sharp Axe)

## 1. The Information-Extraction Phase (The Scan)
* **Goal:** Build a mental map of the territory.
* **Action:** Demand a flat list of keywords and a hierarchy of concepts.
* **Logic:** Vocabulary is the leverage; without it, you cannot communicate effectively with the AI or yourself.

## 2. The Logic of Analogy (The Mental Model)
* **Goal:** Move from memorization to intuition.
* **Action:** Bridge technical properties to familiar concepts (e.g., Aperture vs. Film Slide).
* **Logic:** If you understand the physics, the syntax becomes secondary.

## 3. The Deliberate Stress-Test (The Lab)
* **Goal:** Find the "Breaking Points."
* **Action:** Perform Destructive Testing—intentionally break layouts to see where boundaries lie.
* **Logic:** Learning why something disappears or fails defines the true physics of the system.

## 4. The Synthesis & Reflection (The Audit)
* **Goal:** Ensure 1-to-1 Awareness.
* **Action:** Articulate the concept back to the AI partner in your own words.
* **Logic:** This is the Lead Domino. It prevents "drifting" into un-clarity before adding more complexity.

## 5. Velocity Optimization (The 75-Minute Target)
* **Pre-flight:** Assets must be atomic and ready before the session.
* **Micro-Sprints:** 5m Insight -> 10m Breaking Point -> 10m Synthesis.
* **Feedback Loop:** Front-load the glossary to skip what is already intuitively understood.
