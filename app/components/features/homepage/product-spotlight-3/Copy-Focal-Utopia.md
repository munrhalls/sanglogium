# Focal — Acoustic Zenith
## The Absolute Pinnacle

The Utopia stands at the unrivaled peak of high-fidelity engineering. Featuring pure Beryllium "M"-shaped dome drivers in a specialized open-back architecture, it offers a listening experience of staggering dynamic range and crystalline purity.

Aesthetic perfection handcrafted in France. Sculpted with recycled forged carbon yokes and precision honeycomb grilles, the Utopia is the ultimate lightweight masterpiece for those who demand the world’s finest sonic signature.
