# Dan Clark — Engineering Purity
## The Reference Icon

The Stealth represents the definitive pursuit of the "blackest" background in high-end audio. Utilizing a proprietary Acoustic Metamaterial Tuning System (AMTS), it eliminates standing waves to reveal a soundstage depth and transparency previously thought impossible for a closed-back design.

Where complexity meets invisibility. With its self-adjusting suspension and ultra-lightweight carbon fiber chassis, the Stealth disappears during use, leaving only the raw, unfiltered emotion of the master recording.
