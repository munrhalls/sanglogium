# Prevent scope creep
- Scope about crystal clear first
- DoD about crystal clear first
- Each step: one small thing
- Each step: definition of done
- Each step: rough realistic time scope
- Layers discerned
- Layers sequenced

**Why** 
These prevent over-extending (scope creep), taking on too much at once (scope creep), tackling multiple different stage steps at once (scope creep in when), endless improvement (no stop condition), endless refinement (no stop condition) - all of which almost automatically guarantee failure.

Lack of layers discernment means mixing up layers from different domains where each domain has its own problems - combined, its exponentially more complex - almost automatically guarantees failure.

Lack of layers sequencing means working on layers out of order, which creates dependencies issues and rework, which is exponential complexity explosion and time waste - almost automatically gurantees failure. 

**Mindset habits**

# racing but not rushing
Every second compounds. If not racing this second, not racing the next, then minute, hour, day passes with nothing done. Slack becomes more slack becomes nothing. If rushing this second...same compound to rushed, more rushed...entangled, lost and nothing done. If calmly racing with alert, serene watchfulness, accuracy compounds to coherent done. Compound effect either slackens to nothing, entangles to rushed nothing, or tightens to coherent done.

# let expressing my highest potential but not forcing
Forcing equals stifling. Rushing equals vodoo blindfold rush to nowhere. Let means...not getting in the way.

# letting but not dragging
Letting so much that it's slack covered under unicorn running the clouds to cosmic nirvana of nothing. Letting is uncontrolled but balanced. Letting equals Connor McGregor in the ring - serene look on his face but underneath, it's all balanced aggression, alert and calm, precise...ready, relaxed and on point.
