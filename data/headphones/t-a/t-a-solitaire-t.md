## CONFLICTS
- **batteryLifeHours**: The product page states "Battery Lifetime 70 h, 35 in HQ mode"; the user manual technical data states "Battery Lifetime 40 h, 28 h in HQ mode". The live product page is the more current manufacturer source, but neither source provides an ANC-on/ANC-off split, so both `ancOff` and `ancOn` are recorded as null. — https://www.ta-hifi.de/en/headphones/solitaire-t/ & https://www.ta-hifi.de/wp-content/uploads/solitaire_t_DE_EN_web.pdf
- **freqResponseHz**: Product page lists "active 4 - 22,000 Hz, passive 4-45,000 Hz"; manual lists "4 - 22,000 Hz". Resolved by recording the wider passive range (4-45,000 Hz), which T+A headlines for passive wired mode; the active 4-22,000 Hz range is retained here. — https://www.ta-hifi.de/en/headphones/solitaire-t/ & https://www.ta-hifi.de/wp-content/uploads/solitaire_t_DE_EN_web.pdf
- **cableLengthM**: Manual lists an analog 1.4 m cable, a balanced 3 m cable and a USB-C cable. The schema has a single value, so the shorter mobile analog cable (1.4 m) is recorded; the 3 m balanced cable and USB-C cable are noted here. — https://www.ta-hifi.de/wp-content/uploads/solitaire_t_DE_EN_web.pdf
