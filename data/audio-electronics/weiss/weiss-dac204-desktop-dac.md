## Conflict / Caution Notes

- `finishColor` has a same-tier conflict: the Stereophile reprint lists "Finish: Silver" for the reviewed unit, while the HFN reprint (hosted by the manufacturer) and Bluebird Music describe a "black chassis/casework with a silver faceplate/fascia". Asia Weiss lists "Available Color: Silver". The value `["Black", "Silver"]` records both colors found in the sources rather than forcing a single-color resolution.
- The live weiss.ch product page at `/products/highend-hifi/dac204/` now serves the DAC204-MK2, because the original DAC204 is no longer available. The manufacturer manual and blog cover both the original DAC204 and the DAC204-MK2 and state that the MK2 is "identical to the initial version" in connectivity and all other features except the upgraded DAC board/output stage. Sourced values that apply to both versions (inputs, sample rates, dimensions, finish, country of manufacture) are cited from the manual or the live page; the original chip (`ESS Sabre 9018S`) is sourced from the Stereophile reprint because the manufacturer page is silent on the original chip.
