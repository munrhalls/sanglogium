## Conflict / Caution Notes

- **Product-category mismatch with the current schema:** The DHA V590² is marketed as a headphone amplifier, DAC, and preamplifier. The should-be-audio-electronics.md product-category list does not contain a combined "headphone DAC/amp" or "DAC/preamp" value, so it was filed as `deviceType: dac`. This is the single best fit, but it renders several amplification-related fields (`outputs`, `remoteControlIncluded`, `amplification`, `powerOutputPerChannelW`, `channelCount`) non-applicable (`null`) under the current schema domain rules, even though the hardware has headphone outputs, line outputs, a remote, and a solid-state amplifier.
- **Power output:** The source documents quote headphone-output power (6,400 mW Pmax at 50 Ω / 21 V RMS at 600 Ω). This was not recorded in `powerOutputPerChannelW` because that field is defined for speaker-amplifier W RMS output, not headphone output power.
