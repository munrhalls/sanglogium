## Conflict / Caution Notes

- **Outputs schema-domain mismatch:** WANDLA HP has real balanced XLR, unbalanced RCA line outputs, and a 4.4 mm / 6.35 mm headphone output, but the current `outputs` field in `sanity-cms/schemaTypes/productType.ts` is domain-gated to the five amplifier/receiver `deviceType` values. Because `deviceType` is `dac`, `outputs` is recorded as `null`; the real output connectors and headphone power are documented in the notes instead.
- **Headphone output power vs. `powerOutputPerChannelW`:** The product page and manual list significant headphone output power (3.5 W balanced into 50 Ω, 1 W single-ended into 50 Ω). However, `powerOutputPerChannelW` is domain-gated to amplifier/receiver `deviceType` values and its semantics are speaker-channel-oriented, so the headphone power is not recorded in `powerOutputPerChannelW`; it is documented here.
